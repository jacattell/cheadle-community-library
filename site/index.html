Computers

Free access for library members:

  • 8 bookable computers over 2 floors
  • high speed internet access
  • LibreOffice (Microsoft Office alternative)
  • A4 Canon CanoScan LiDE 300 scanner on computer number 7 upstairs

Print and photocopy

We have an HP Colour LaserJet Managed MFP E786 printer / photocopier.

Currently you can only print via one of the library computers, or by plugging your USB-A memory stick into the printer.

Our volunteers will gladly help.
