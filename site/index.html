Print and photocopy

We have HP Colour LaserJet Managed MFP E786 printer / photocopier. Currently you can only print via one of the library computers, or by plugging your USB-A Memory stick into the printer. Our volunteers will gladly help.

The costs per page are:

A4 black & white15p
A4 colour50p
A3 black & white30p
A3 colour£1
